# Clock 0.2.0

Android 12 or newer. No internet permission, no analytics, no ads.

## New

- **Duplicate alarms.** In the alarm editor, tap the copy icon: the screen becomes a new alarm with
  everything on it, the original stays as it was, and the copy is only created when you save.
  Or long-press alarms in the list and tap **Duplicate**: copies are created straight away,
  switched off, so they never ring together with the original.

## Fixed

- The multi-select bar now fits all its actions; long labels shrink instead of being cut.

Download `Clock-0.2.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. It updates 0.1.0 in place and keeps
