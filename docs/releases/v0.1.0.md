# Clock 0.1.0

First public build. Android 12 or newer. No internet permission, no analytics, no ads.

## What's in it

- **Alarms that always ring**: exact alarm-clock scheduling, a full-screen alarm over the lock
  screen (also before the first unlock after a reboot), rescheduling after reboots, updates and
  time or time zone changes, missed-alarm notices, and a reliability screen with steps for each
  manufacturer.
- **Alarms**: once, weekly or on chosen dates; single use; skip next; notices before an alarm
  (minutes, hours or days, several at once) with "Dismiss this one"; groups with a master switch;
  multi-select and batch edit; collision warnings; trash with undo; time entry by keyboard or
  wheels.
- **Sound**: system tones or your own files, gradual volume, vibration patterns or vibration synced
  to the sound, preset or custom ring duration, phone + Bluetooth output, low-volume warning,
  volume-down to silence a ringing alarm.
- **Alarm screen**: tap Snooze, or hold it and slide over a dial to pick another length (your own
  snooze length included); slide to dismiss; a library of layouts with an editor (clock styles,
  seven fonts, weight, colours, date format, solid, gradient, photo, silent video or animated
  backgrounds) and a live "Test".
- **Time zones**: a zone per alarm, a manual app zone, and both times shown.
- **World clock**, **timers** (several at once, live notifications, full screen when they end,
  +1 min) and a **stopwatch** with laps.
- English and European Portuguese; light and dark themes; five accent presets or any colour;
  "liquid glass" floating controls; haptic feedback throughout.

## Known limits

- On Android 16, large screens (600 dp and wider) may still rotate the app, which is otherwise
  portrait only.
- Samsung delivers boot broadcasts about a minute late; an alarm due in that minute after a reboot
  is reported as missed.
