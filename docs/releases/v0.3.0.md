# Clock 0.3.0

Android 12 or newer. No internet permission, no analytics, no ads.

## New: widgets

- **Clock**: the time and date on your home screen.
- **Next alarm**: when and what rings next ("Tomorrow · Gym", "Rings in 7 h 30 min"), a
  **Skip next** button (and "Don't skip"), and a warning when a phone setting may keep alarms from
  ringing.
- **Style them however you like**: one line or stacked, any of the app's seven fonts, weight, dot
  shape, separator, colours (automatic, accent or any colour), a solid, gradient or photo
  background, opacity, corners, alignment and margins, or one of seven ready-made styles. The
  editor shows a live preview for light and dark home screens.
- Drawn with the app's own fonts and kept current every minute while the screen is on (nothing
  runs while it is off). They switch with the home screen's light or dark theme.
- **Settings > Widgets** adds them to the home screen and edits them; you can also hold a widget
  and open its settings.
