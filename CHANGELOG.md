# Changelog

All notable changes to Clock. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## Unreleased

## [0.2.0](https://github.com/iM-lemoS/Clock/releases/tag/v0.2.0) (2026-10-24)

- Duplicate alarms: from the editor (opens an unsaved copy with everything on screen) or from
  multi-select (copies start switched off, so they never ring together with the original).
- The multi-select bar fits six actions; long labels shrink instead of being cut.

## [0.1.0](https://github.com/iM-lemoS/Clock/releases/tag/v0.1.0) (2026-10-24)

First public build. Full notes: [`docs/releases/v0.1.0.md`](docs/releases/v0.1.0.md).

- Alarms that always ring: exact scheduling, full screen over the lock screen, direct boot,
  rescheduling after reboots, updates and time changes, missed-alarm notices, and a reliability
  screen with per-manufacturer steps.
- Recurrence (once, weekdays, dates), single use, skip next, notices before alarms, groups, batch
  edit, collisions and trash.
- Sound, vibration, ring duration and snooze per alarm, with a snooze dial on the alarm screen.
- Alarm screen layouts with an editor and a library.
- Per-alarm time zones, world clock, timers and stopwatch.
- English and European Portuguese, light and dark themes, accent colours, liquid glass.
